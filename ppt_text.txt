TECHNICAL APPROACH & SYSTEM PIPELINEEnd-to-End Technical Flow: Earth Observation, Dual Machine Learning & Prescriptive MILP
1. DATA HARVESTING
• Spaceborne EO Feeds : Sentinel-2 L2A (B2, B4, B8, B11, B12), ASTER Level 1T, Copernicus 30m DEM.• Meteorological Intelligence : Open-Meteo & IMD Pune gridded 72h precipitation, soil moisture & atmospheric pressure.• Pit Operations Logs : Dumper cycle times, shovel payload logs, crusher weighbridge telemetry.• Processing Pipeline : Google Earth Engine (GEE) & GDAL for cloud masking, radiometric calibration & raster tiling.
2. DUAL AI / ML ENGINES
• Upstream Exploration (MPM) : Calculates diagnostic spectral ratios: Iron Oxide (B4/B2), Ferrous Silicate (B11/B8), Hydroxyl (B11/B12).• Prospectivity Classifier : Random Forest Classifier maps 0.0–1.0 Mineral Prospectivity Index (MPI) GeoTIFF from GSI ground truth.• Downstream Operations Forecaster : XGBoost Multivariate Regressor maps pit telemetry & rainfall saturation index to predict 3–7 day production shortfalls (ΔTt).• Model Explainability : TreeSHAP explicitly attributes risk percentages (e.g. 65% haul slippage, 35% shovel queue).
3. PRESCRIPTIVE DISPATCH
• Optimization Framework : Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) solved via Google OR-Tools & PuLP.• Dynamic Safety Cutoff : Zero haulage dispatched to benches where precipitation >35mm/24h or slope hazard score >0.75.• Grade Blending Constraint : Enforces crusher feed balance: Σ(Qi · Gi) / ΣQi = 38% ± 1.5% Mn under varying moisture.• Prescriptive Output : Delivers real-time dumper reroutes away from inundated benches to dry stockpiles, preventing crusher starvation.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITYOperational Feasibility, Economic Viability, Technical Challenges & Mitigation Matrix
FEASIBILITY :
• Technical : Lightweight, proven ML algorithms (Random Forest + XGBoost) train in minutes on standard cloud/edge CPUs without requiring expensive GPU clusters.• Data Compliance : 100% open-access Earth Observation data (Copernicus Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1 SAR, USGS ASTER) — zero recurring data licensing fees.• Operational : Pilots immediately as a non-intrusive software dashboard using existing MOIL shift spreadsheets and weighbridge logs before fleet hardware retrofits.• Economic : Zero initial sensor capex; eliminates speculative borehole drilling expenses saving ₹30L–₹50L per exploration phase.
VIABILITY :
• Direct MOIL ROI : Preventing just 3 days of weather-induced crusher starvation saves ₹60 Lakhs to ₹1.2 Crore per mine pit annually.• Capital Efficiency : Cuts speculative diamond-core drilling by 30–40% via high-probability satellite prospectivity targeting.• Policy Alignment : Directly supports India's National Mineral Policy 2019 and Ministry of Steel's self-reliance mandate for domestic manganese reserves.• Multi-Pit Scalability : Calibrated on Central Indian Manganese Belts (Balaghat, Dongri Buzurg, Mansar, Gumgaon); rapidly expandable to chromite, bauxite & iron pits.
TECHNICAL CHALLENGES :
Risk 1 (Monsoon Cloud Occlusion) : Optical Sentinel-2/ASTER bands are blocked by heavy monsoon clouds during peak rainfall.Mitigation : Automated switch to Sentinel-1 C-Band SAR radar data which penetrates cloud cover to monitor surface moisture and pit slope stability.
Risk 2 (Pit Multipath & Deadzones) : Deep open-cast pits cause wireless sensor signal drops and packet loss.Mitigation : Edge-logging telemetry units cache data locally on haulers, opportunistically syncing via solar-powered Wi-Fi mesh skids on pit rims.
BUSINESS / USER CHALLENGES :
Risk 1 (Field Dispatcher Distrust) : Pit supervisors accustomed to manual radio calls may reject automated algorithmic directives.Mitigation : Integrated TreeSHAP Explainable AI explicitly details the physical rationale (e.g. 'Bench 2 closed due to 82% mud-slip risk; rerouting to dry Bench 4 preserves 38% Mn').
Risk 2 (Data Heterogeneity Across Pits) : Different MOIL lease sites use varying log formats and shift tracking sheets.Mitigation : Automated ETL ingestion pipeline with schema validation and robust fallback defaults for missing telemetry fields.
